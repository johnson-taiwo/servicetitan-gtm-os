# Brand voice - ServiceTitan (from `context/docs/research/voice.md`)

| Element | ServiceTitan's own words |
|---|---|
| Headline | "AI software for commercial and residential trades" |
| Claim | "The #1 software for commercial and residential trades" |
| Category (2026) | "AI for the trades - a purpose-built agentic operating system" |
| Section line | "Build your legacy with ServiceTitan." / "Built for the Trades, Built for Your Business" |
| How they name customers | Filings: "the trades", "trades businesses". Web: "contractors". Founders: "hard-working contractors", "hardworking men and women of the trades" |
| Staff | "Titans" |

**Tone (INFERRED from the above):** respectful of the trades and the people in them, growth- and legacy-minded, plain
and confident, proud to be built only for contractors. Speaks to owners as business builders, not as "users".
**Do:** "contractors", "the trades", "technicians", concrete numbers from customers. **Don't:** tech jargon, "users",
anything that talks down to tradespeople, em dashes in our own copy.
