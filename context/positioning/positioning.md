# Positioning - ServiceTitan

*From `context/docs/research/`. Their words in quotes; our synthesis marked INFERRED.*

| Element | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Category | "AI software for commercial and residential trades"; recently "AI for the trades - a purpose-built agentic operating system" | vo (homepage, Sep-Oct 2026) |
| Target customer | Trades businesses that are growing, "from four trucks to thousands"; not "down-market trades businesses ... five employees or fewer" | sg §1 #1; co (IPO) |
| Alternative they use today | Paper, spreadsheets, QuickBooks, many disconnected tools, or a starter app (Jobber, Housecall Pro, Service Fusion) | cu §5 |
| Unique value | Built only for the trades, end to end (calls, dispatch, field, payments, reporting, marketing) on one platform, with AI agents (Atlas, Max) on top | co; cmp §3 ("100% Custom Built For Serving the Trades") |
| Proof | About 10,800 Active Customers, $82.1B gross transaction volume (FY2026), named customer results | co; cu §2 |

**Positioning statement (INFERRED, our wording):** For trade contractors who are growing past what paper and starter
apps can run, ServiceTitan is the operating system for the whole business, from the first call to the final payment.
Unlike starter apps, it is built to scale with more technicians and locations; unlike horizontal field-service
software, it is built only for the trades.
