# Value proposition - ServiceTitan, per persona

*From `context/docs/research/` (tags as in `context/icp/icp.md`). Proof is quoted or numbered exactly as in the source.*

| Persona | Job to be done | Pain (their words) | Gain | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner** (economic buyer) | Grow revenue and profit without the owner holding the business together by hand | "I'm not just a plumber's wife answering phones anymore" (cu §3, Young Plumbing, before and after); "That's the difference of paying the bills or not" (cu §3, Redding) | Revenue growth with the same or fewer office hours; numbers they trust | Young Plumbing $1.2M to $2.1M, 4 to 8 trucks in a year (cu §2); Boss Roofing $500,000 overhead saved last year (cu §2); ServiceTitan says customers grew revenue "21% in their first two years" (cu §1) |
| **Ops / General Manager** (champion) | Keep every technician on profitable jobs as the crew grows | "We felt like we were driving the bus through the rearview mirror" (cu §3, Crown); "10 different softwares" (cu §5, Boss Roofing) | One system for calls, dispatch, jobs, invoices and reporting; job costing | Interstate AC +33% revenue first full year, billing 2 to 3 weeks faster (cu §2); Esser Air 50% fewer callbacks in year one (cu §2) |
| **Office Manager / CSR lead** (influencer) | Answer every call, fill the schedule, get invoices and payroll out | "I used to work 100 hours a week" (cu §3, Esser Air CSR) | Booking and after-hours calls handled; invoices and payroll off spreadsheets | Brothers Plumbing abandon rate "near 30%" to 5%, $1.33M attributed revenue (cu §2); Crown payroll team 3 to 1 (cu §2); Adams Power invoices in 1.5 to 2 weeks vs up to 4 (cu §3) |
