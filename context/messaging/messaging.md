# Messaging - ServiceTitan

*From `context/positioning/` and `context/docs/research/`. Words to use come from ServiceTitan and its customers.*

**Core message:** Run the whole trades business on one system, so growth adds trucks, not chaos.

| Pillar | What we say | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Every call booked | Calls answered and booked, after hours too, as the phone load grows | Brothers Plumbing abandon rate "near 30%" to 5% (cu §2); Vines abandoned calls to "slim to none" (cu §2) |
| 2. Numbers you can trust | Job costing and live reports instead of guessing | Crown "driving the bus through the rearview mirror" before (cu §3); Interstate AC WIP report "in an instant" (cu §3) |
| 3. Cash in faster | Invoices and payments out in days, not weeks | Adams Power 1.5 to 2 weeks vs up to 4 (cu §3); Vision Garage Doors payment 36 days to 5 (cu §2) |
| 4. Grow without the office growing as fast | Add technicians and locations on the same system | Young Plumbing 4 to 8 trucks in a year (cu §2); Crown payroll team 3 to 1 (cu §2) |

| Persona | Angle |
|---|---|
| Owner | Growth without holding it together by hand; numbers and cash |
| Ops / GM | Technicians busy on the right jobs; one board, one set of numbers |
| Office lead | Every call booked; billing and payroll off spreadsheets |

**Words to use:** contractors, the trades, technicians, trucks, calls booked, jobs, memberships, job costing, "built for
the trades" (vo). **Words to avoid:** "disrupt", "revolutionize", "cheap", any price or contract claim, anything that
says their business is failing.
