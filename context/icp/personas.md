# Personas - ServiceTitan

*From `context/docs/research/`. Tags as in `icp.md` (cu = customers.md, sg = signals.md, co = company.md, pr =
practitioner.md). Quotes are word for word from the source. In shops under about 20 staff, the owner often plays two
or three of these roles.*

## 1. Champion: Operations or General Manager

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job titles** | General Manager, Operations Manager, Service Manager, Director of Operations, VP of Service; in owner-run shops, the co-owner who runs the office |
| **Measured on** | Jobs completed per technician, call booking rate, average ticket, callbacks, technician utilisation, time to invoice |
| **Top 3 pains, in customers' words** | 1. Blind numbers: "We felt like we were driving the bus through the rearview mirror" (Crown, cu §3). 2. Tool sprawl: "10 different softwares" (Boss Roofing, cu §5). 3. Slow billing: invoices "could be four weeks" before (Adams Power, cu §3) |
| **What makes them start looking** | Adding technicians faster than the office can dispatch; opening a second location; a homegrown or starter system breaking (sg §1 #13, #15) |
| **Objections** | "Training is awful!" and the effort to switch (cu §7); losing sales during the change: "we lost numerous sales because we didn't have the staff required to keep up with the processes" (cu §7) |
| **Where to reach them** | Email and LinkedIn (more likely on LinkedIn than technicians, pr §1); trade associations (sg §1 #8) |

## 2. Economic buyer: the Owner

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job titles** | Owner, President, CEO, Founder, Co-Owner |
| **Measured on** | Revenue growth, net profit, owner hours worked, cash collected, company value at sale |
| **Top 3 pains, in customers' words** | 1. Running the business, not just the jobs: "I'm not just a plumber's wife answering phones anymore. I'm running a business and ServiceTitan is how I do it" (Jessica Young, Co-Owner, Young Plumbing, cu §3). 2. Cash: "That's the difference of paying the bills or not" (Eric Krzywicki, Owner, Redding Heating & Air, cu §3). 3. Systems that hold growth back: "Why didn't I have this 20 years ago?" (Aaron Butler, VP of Service, Karber, cu §3) |
| **What makes them start looking** | A growth goal ServiceTitan's customers describe ("What is your expectation of growth?", Ellington, sg §1 #14); a PE offer or plan to sell (mk §5); a peer's results |
| **Objections** | Price: "We're paying an insane premium and only using a fraction of the features" (cu §7). Lock-in: "I was not clearly told I was entering a 2-year contract" and a reported "$29,000 cancellation fee" (cu §7). Fit: "ServiceTitan was too expensive" for a small company (sg §1 #16) |
| **The decision** | "The key decision maker for our customers is typically the business owner" (sg §1 #5) |

## 3. Influencer: Office Manager or CSR Lead

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job titles** | Office Manager, Customer Service Manager, Lead CSR, Dispatcher, Bookkeeper or Controller (on billing and payroll) |
| **Measured on** | Calls answered and booked, abandoned calls, schedule filled, payroll and invoices on time |
| **Top 3 pains, in customers' words** | 1. Missed calls: abandoned calls "400-500/day" before (Vines, cu §2); "near 30%" abandon rate (Brothers Plumbing, cu §2). 2. Hours: "I used to work 100 hours a week. Now I'm working 40 hours a week" (Tracy Patterson, CSR, Esser Air, cu §3). 3. Payroll and billing by hand: payroll team "3 people to 1" after the switch (Crown, cu §2) |
| **What makes them start looking** | Call volume up after hiring technicians; a busy season (mk §7); a storm week (pr §5) |
| **Objections** | Learning a complex system: "challenging to learn and implement, but worth the effort" (cu §7); support: "I can't get a real person on the phone" (cu §7) |
| **Their role in the deal** | Uses it every day; the owner asks them whether it works. Their quote often becomes the case study (Young Plumbing, Esser Air, cu §3) |
