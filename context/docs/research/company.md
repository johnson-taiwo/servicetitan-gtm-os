## 2. What they sell

### 2a. Platform structure (10-K wording)
- Three offerings: "Core, FinTech and Pro products." Core is "Our entry point to a typical customer's business" (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23).
- Five "core centers of gravity": CRM, FSM, ERP, HCM, FinTech (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23).
- Max: "in fiscal 2026, we began a pilot of Max, which packages our most advanced functionality and AI features with expert guidance to unlock AI automation" (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23).
- Atlas: "an advanced AI sidekick that represents the next evolution of Titan Intelligence, our AI engine"; introduced in fiscal 2026 (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23).
- TitanAdvisor: recommends features "not yet implemented by a customer" (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23).

## 4. Markets and trades served

- Residential trades listed in site menu: "HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Chimney, Roofing, Garage Door, Septic, Pest Control, Irrigation, Painting" (source: https://www.servicetitan.com/, checked 2026-11-23).
- Commercial trades listed in site menu: "Mechanical, Refrigeration, Plumbing, Electrical, Landscaping, Roofing, Kitchen Equipment, Water Treatment, Fire & Life Safety, Dock and Door" (source: https://www.servicetitan.com/, checked 2026-11-23).
- Footer industries: "HVAC Software, Plumbing Software, Electrician Software, Garage Door, Chimney Sweep, Water Treatment, Landscape, Pool Service, Septic, Pest Control, Lawn Care, Roofing" (source: https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-23).
- Solutions menu: Service & Replacement, Construction, Enterprise, Private Equity, Franchise, Sales Prospecting (source: https://www.servicetitan.com/, checked 2026-11-23).
- "we now serve many trades that serve all sites: homes, businesses and even new construction" (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23).
- "We are expanding our sales focus to include large businesses, commercial services and construction customers" (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23).
- Geography: US and Canada core; "significant opportunity to strategically expand the usage of our platform outside of the United States and Canada over time" (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23).
- Market size at IPO: ~$1.5 trillion annual US and Canada trades spend; ~$650 billion "serviceable industry spend"; ~$13 billion "serviceable market opportunity" (source: 424B4, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000119312524277099/d577298d424b4.htm, checked 2026-11-23). Not restated in FY26 10-K (searched "$1.5 trillion": 0 hits).
- Seasonality: hot summer months lift landscaping, pool, pest, HVAC and roofing demand; Q2 is the strongest quarter (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23).
