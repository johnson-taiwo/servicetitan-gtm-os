# Market - ServiceTitan (raw research, checked 2026-11-23)

Scope: regulation and market changes that create urgency for US/Canada trade contractors (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing) to adopt or switch field-service software. Raw facts only. Interpretation is marked INFERRED. Every line carries its source URL and the date checked (2026-11-23).

Legend for "How verified": PAGE = page opened and text read this session. FILE = official data file downloaded and read. SEARCH = seen in a search-engine summary only; the page itself refused automated fetch, so treat as lower confidence.

## 1. HVAC refrigerant transition (EPA AIM Act, R-410A, A2L)

| # | Fact | Source (checked 2026-11-23) | How verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The AIM Act (enacted in the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021) "authorizes EPA to address hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs) by providing new authorities in three main areas: to phase down the production and consumption of listed HFCs, manage these HFCs and their substitutes, and facilitate the transition to next-generation technologies through sector-based restrictions." | https://www.epa.gov/climate-hfcs-reduction/aim-act | PAGE |
| 2 | HFC phasedown target: 85% below historic baseline by 2036. Cap steps as share of baseline: 90% (2022-2023), 60% (2024-2028), 30% (2029-2033), 20% (2034-2035), 15% (2036 on). | https://epa.gov/climate-hfcs-reduction/hfc-allowance-allocation-and-reporting ; https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2023-07/SAN-8838-Final-Rule_Fact-Shee_508.pdf | SEARCH |
| 3 | EPA Technology Transitions table: "Stationary residential and light commercial air conditioning and heat pumps" have a GWP limit of 700 with a manufacture and import compliance date of January 1, 2025. | https://www.epa.gov/climate-hfcs-reduction/technology-transitions-hfc-restrictions-sector | PAGE |
| 4 | Same EPA table, installation side: "Residential and light commercial air conditioning and heat pump systems (e.g., mini-splits, unitary systems)" GWP limit 700, date January 1, 2025, with "No installation compliance date for systems where all specified components were manufactured or imported prior to January 1, 2025". | https://www.epa.gov/climate-hfcs-reduction/technology-transitions-hfc-restrictions-sector | PAGE |
| 5 | Original rule: installation of pre-2025 R-410A residential split systems was to end January 1, 2026. EPA published a final rule (NAHB post dated May 26, 2026) "that will allow the continued installation in homes of HVAC units manufactured or imported prior to Jan. 1, 2025, that use R-410A refrigerant until existing supplies of these HVAC units are depleted. The rule becomes effective on July 27, 2026." | https://www.nahb.org/blog/2026/05/epa-hvac-refrigerants-r-410a-final-rule | PAGE |
| 6 | State exception: "New York has passed a statewide law codifying the original Jan. 1, 2026 deadline, meaning no R-410A systems can be installed in New York." | https://www.nahb.org/blog/2026/05/epa-hvac-refrigerants-r-410a-final-rule | PAGE |
| 7 | R-410A "is being gradually phased out on a schedule that will decrease its production year-by-year until it reaches 15% of current output by 2036. This will impact supply of R-410A used for maintenance going forward." | https://www.nahb.org/blog/2026/05/epa-hvac-refrigerants-r-410a-final-rule | PAGE |
| 8 | EPA's Sept 2025 reconsideration proposal cited supply chain and A2L availability concerns; ACHR News headline "EPA removes R-410A installation deadline". | https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-09/sept-2025-technology-transitions-reconsideration-proposal-fact-sheet.pdf ; https://www.achrnews.com/articles/166226-epa-removes-r-410a-installation-deadline | SEARCH (ACHR returned 403) |
| 9 | Variable refrigerant flow (VRF) systems have a different date: installation allowed until January 1, 2027 for certain new VRF systems using components imported before January 1, 2026 (Dec 2024 rule). | https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-12-12/html/2024-29243.htm | SEARCH |
| 10 | 2025 A2L supply problem (R-454B): Facilities Dive, May 28, 2025: "Shortages of A2L-compliant refrigerant R454-B are expected to persist through the third quarter". Quoted price: "The cost of refrigerant has surged to $60 per pound, compared to $17 per pound in p[rior...]" (quote cut at page break). | https://www.facilitiesdive.com/news/r-32-touted-as-a2l-compliant-refrigerant-while-r-454b-shortage-persists/749146/ | PAGE |
| 11 | ACHR News (Oct 13, 2025 opinion) reported industry voices calling the worst of the R-454B shortage "officially over"; the shortage was of cylinders, not refrigerant in pre-charged equipment. | https://www.achrnews.com/blogs/17-opinions/post/165373-hardi-on-r-454b-the-crisis-is-over | SEARCH (403) |

INFERRED: The federal "hard stop" on R-410A installs that drove urgency in 2025 was softened in May 2026 (fact 5), except in New York (fact 6). Remaining urgency drivers are tighter R-410A service supply (fact 7) and A2L handling for new installs.
