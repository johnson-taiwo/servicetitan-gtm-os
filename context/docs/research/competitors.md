## 1. Who ServiceTitan itself names as competitors

- 10-K (FY ended 2026-01-31): "Examples of these software vendors include Salesforce, SAP, FieldEdge, Workwave, ServiceTrade, AccuLynx, BuildOps, HouseCall Pro, JobNimbus and Jobber." Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1638826/000163882626000028/ttan-20260131.htm, checked 2026-11-23.
- 10-K groups them as: "software vendors offering point-specific tools for specific elements of trade workflows, horizontal solutions for generic functionalities, legacy on-premise field service management applications and narrow bundled solutions for down-market trades businesses." Same source, 2026-11-23.
- 10-K on its own edge: "we compete favorably across key industry selection criteria, including our cloud-based platform connectivity, ease of deployment and integration, user experience, industry expertise, product breadth, customer support, proven success record, mobile capabilities, and innovation capacity". Same source, 2026-11-23.
