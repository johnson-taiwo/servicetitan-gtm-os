# Campaign Strategy for ServiceTitan

*Built 23 Nov 2026 with the campaign-strategy skill method, from `CLAUDE.md`, `context/` and
`knowledge-base/playbooks/workflow-anatomy.md`. Base list for every idea: US trade contractors in the ICP
(`context/icp/icp.md`), size proven at 10+ unless the idea says otherwise. Volumes are estimates (INFERRED) until a
search count runs. Customer names in copy only once they are approved in `context/messaging/facts.md`.*

## Customer Discovery Analysis

From the 30 named customers (`context/docs/research/customers.md`):

- **Size range in practice:** $1.2M (Young Plumbing) to $140M+ (Del-Air). Most stories sit at $2M to $25M with 4 to 70
  trucks. The stated floor is "four trucks in a single shop" (signals.md §1 #1).
- **Trades:** HVAC and plumbing dominate; electrical, roofing, garage door and restoration all have stories.
- **What they switched from:** paper, spreadsheets and whiteboards (6 of 18 stories), many disconnected tools, QuickBooks,
  Jobber (Vines). Only two stories name the old vendor.
- **What they measure after:** calls booked and abandoned, close rate, average ticket, invoice speed, payroll time,
  memberships, revenue growth.
- **Success outside the stated core:** roofing ($1M to $3M+ Home Run Roofing, Boss Roofing $14M to $26M), garage doors
  in Canada (Canadoor 350% growth, Vision ticket $80 to $800), restoration (Vines), commercial (Interstate AC, Adams
  Power). These justify the lookalike and Canada ideas below.

**Best customers for lookalike campaigns:** Young Plumbing (small residential plumbing, 4 to 8 trucks), Redding
Heating & Air (HVAC, second location), Best Quality Electric (electrical, 3 to 9 trucks), Home Run Roofing (roofing, $1M
to $3M+), Interstate AC (commercial HVAC and plumbing).

## Section 2: No-AI campaign

**"Hiring a dispatcher?"** Three short emails to owners whose post is for a dispatcher (cell C3/C4 list), static copy,
no personalisation beyond first name, company and city. Works without AI because the list itself is the message: the
job post says scheduling has become a full-time job. Structure: one-line observation (the role), one-line outcome (one
board for jobs and technicians), one proof line, a yes/no question.

## Section 3: Front-end offer suggestions (INFERRED, need ServiceTitan's OK)

1. **Missed-call math:** a one-page estimate from numbers they give (calls a week, share unanswered, average ticket).
   Shows the value of booking every call before any demo. Fits W1 C1 and C2.
2. **First 90 days checklist for a new GM:** what to map in calls, dispatch, billing and reporting. Fits the New Hire
   campaign; useful even if they never buy.
3. **Local owner roundtable:** a small dinner for owners in one metro with a nearby customer speaking. Mirrors how the
   trades trust peers (the customer stories are owner to owner).
